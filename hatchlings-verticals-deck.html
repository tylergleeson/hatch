VERTICAL 01Fragrance

Blind Accord

“You can’t smell it before you buy it.”

A perfumer releases numbered series; each sealed flacon is a blind pull across a rarity spread — house accords, rare limited macerations, and a single 1/1 “Perfumer’s Reserve” batch. Serial-etched glass. Build a fragrance wardrobe; layer them; trade the discontinued ones.

The pull
The reveal is olfactory — you wear it to find out who you got
Tiers
Accord common · Maceration 1:20 · Reserve 1/1
Why it sells
~80% margins, deeply emotional, infinitely re-collectable
Proof
Discontinued scents resell 5–10×; decant culture is huge
The compulsion

A scent you love but can never buy again is the purest grail there is.

size guaranteed · edition unknown
VERTICAL 02Apparel

Blind Editions

“Your size is guaranteed. The edition isn’t.”

You choose your fit; the colorway, print and dye are the blind pull — house colors common, seasonal prints rare, hand-dyed 1/1 grails. A woven, numbered tag authenticates each. Removing the fit-gamble kills the one reason blind apparel normally fails — only the aesthetic is left to chance.

The pull
Variable reward with the return-risk engineered out
Tiers
House common · Seasonal print 1:10 · Hand-dyed 1/1
Why it sells
Streetwear already lives on scarcity, hype & resale
Proof
Sneaker resale alone is a $10B+ market
The compulsion

You’d never gamble on a size. You’ll happily gamble on a colorway.

unroll to find what you own
VERTICAL 03Art & Prints

The Edition Lottery

“Unroll it to find out what you own.”

Sealed limited prints where you don’t know the work, state or colorway you’ll receive. Tiers run open edition → numbered limited → artist proof → the 1/1 hand-finished original. An embossed COA is the serial. Collect a wall; proofs appreciate and trade.

The pull
The unrolling reveal; the dream of pulling the proof
Tiers
Open · Limited /100 · Artist Proof 1:50 · Original 1/1
Why it sells
Wall-worthy, high-margin, with built-in COA authentication
Proof
Hyped print drops resell 3–10× within hours
The compulsion

It’s the only collectible that doubles as the décor you show off daily.

the desk-flex economy
VERTICAL 04Desk & EDC

Blind Artisan

“This economy already runs on the chase.”

Blind drops of hand-sculpted desk objects — keycaps, dice, pen finials, EDC fidgets — across rarity tiers: colorways common, sculpts rare, 1/1 “fauxtisan” grails. Numbered and signed. The enthusiast communities already trade these rabidly; we bring a real brand and fair odds.

The pull
A tiny, jewel-like object you flex on your desk daily
Tiers
Colorway common · Sculpt 1:24 · Fauxtisan 1/1
Why it sells
Devoted communities, high margins, endless variants
Proof
Single artisan keycaps have sold for $1,000+
The compulsion

We’re not creating a chase here — we’re professionalising one that already exists.

every bag 88+ · chase the 92
VERTICAL 05Coffee & Tea

The Cupping Lottery

“Every bag scores 88+. The chase is the 92.”

Monthly sealed micro-lots; you don’t know the origin or varietal until you brew. Rarity is the cupping score and the scarcity — common washed lots, rare naturals and varietals, and a 1/1 auction lot (Best-of-Panama geisha tier). Collect the origin map; trade the rare bags.

The pull
It’s consumable — the gamble comes back every single month
Tiers
Washed common · Varietal 1:12 · Auction lot 1/1
Why it sells
Recurring revenue + frequent, low-stakes dopamine
Proof
Geisha auction lots have cleared $1,000+/lb
The compulsion

A consumable you re-buy is the only collectible with a built-in monthly heartbeat.

The Real Business

One engine. Five shelves.

  • A reusable rarity engine — tiers, pity timers, published odds
  • Serial authentication — a coin, a tag, a COA, an etch
  • A trading marketplace that takes a cut of every resale
  • One collector account that follows the buyer across categories

Build the chase infrastructure once, plug in any vertical. We stop being a perfume company or a coffee company — we become the chase company, and every shelf cross-sells the same collector.
