MECHANIC 01

Grail Ladder

“Every box could be the one-of-one.”

A published rarity ladder: 13 Commons, Rare morphs, Super-Rare anomalies (miscolors, glows, albinos), and a single 1-of-1 Prototype per series. Every unit is serial-numbered with a sealed brass authentication coin — low serials and anomalies command the resale premium.

The hit
The jackpot chase + the near-miss; reading your serial is a scratch-ticket moment
Odds
Rare 1:8 · Anomaly 1:96 · Prototype 1:∞
Why it sells
Built-in resale & status; grading culture; scarcity that appreciates
Margin
A brass coin costs cents; perceived value is enormous
The compulsion

It turns a $15 figure into a lottery ticket with a collectible attached.

5 per pack · 1 guaranteed hit
MECHANIC 02

The Booster

“Open five. One is guaranteed to hit.”

Sold as a sealed pack of five mini-hatchlings, with one slot guaranteed Rare or better. Foil and “parallel” finishes are the chase layer. The whole format is engineered for the pack-opening ritual — and for the camera.

The hit
Five jolts of suspense per purchase, plus the guaranteed-but-which tension
Chase
Foil parallels 1:12, full-art “alt” 1:48
Why it sells
Bigger basket size; the single most-copied structure in collectibles
Free marketing
Made for pack-opening videos — viewers become buyers
The compulsion

TCGs proved it for 25 years: a guaranteed rare keeps you opening; the chase keeps you buying.

own the machine · buy the luck
MECHANIC 03

The Pull Engine

“Own the machine. Buy the luck.”

A beautiful desk gachapon — the showpiece keepsake — fed by sealed, randomized cartridges on subscription. The app logs your collection and shows live odds. The crank is the ritual; the dopamine lives on your desk, not in a checkout flow.

The hit
A tactile, repeatable, at-home pull you control
Model
Razor-and-blades: device + refills on subscription
Why it sells
Recurring revenue & high LTV; the device anchors the brand on the desk
Cadence
A monthly cartridge = scheduled dopamine
The compulsion

The casino lives in your home, and you paid to install it.

millions of combos · a few are legendary
MECHANIC 04

Trait Genetics

“No two alike. A few are legendary.”

Each creature is finished from a randomized roll of trait modules — species, pelt, pattern, eyes, a mutation, an accessory. Millions of combinations, so yours is effectively one-off. A printed trait sheet scores every trait’s rarity, and certain stacks read as Legendary.

The hit
The trait roll + the dream of a legendary stack
Space
6 trait slots → >2M possible combinations
Why it sells
Near-unique units; “rate my pull” is built-in UGC
Depth
Trading-card parallels × creature genetics, made physical
The compulsion

Everyone owns a different one — so everyone wonders if the next is rarer.

three doubles → one new shot
MECHANIC 05

Dupe Alchemy

“Turn your doubles into another shot.”

Bring or scan your duplicates to fuse them up the ladder — five Commons buy a guaranteed Rare roll; three Rares buy a Super-Rare roll. It kills the number-one blind-box complaint and bolts on a second gamble: the transmute itself.

The hit
Dupe-regret becomes fuel; the sacrifice-and-roll ritual
Rates
5 → 1 Rare · 3 → 1 Super-Rare
Why it sells
Fixes dupes; drives repeat buys to feed the forge
Retention
A reason to keep playing after the shelf is “full”
The compulsion

Nothing you pull is ever wasted — which means there’s always a reason to pull again.

The Premium Moat

A chase you can trust.

  • Published odds on every box — printed and in-app, no hidden rates
  • A pity timer — a Super-Rare guaranteed within 30 boxes, so the chase always ends
  • Adults only — positioned and priced for kidults, never marketed to children
  • No dark patterns — no fake scarcity timers, no pay-to-reveal traps

Regulators are already circling hidden loot-box odds. Transparency isn’t a compliance cost here — it’s the brand position: the collectible that respects the collector. The thrill of the chase, played honestly, is the version adults will pay a premium for and trust their friends with.

The angle

Everyone else hides the math. We make the fairness the flex.
